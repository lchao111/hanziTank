# Quartermaster War Prep Icon

## Intended Use

Quartermaster / War Prep button icon for the shop or preparation modal.

## Output Target

- Source: 2048x2048 transparent PNG.
- UI export: 512x512 transparent PNG.
- Optional states: idle, hover glow, pressed dip, ready stamp highlight.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: game-ready 2D UI icon for a war prep / quartermaster button
Primary request: Create a polished quartermaster war-prep icon for TankGame, focused on preparation and equipment management rather than combat.
Scene/backdrop: transparent background with generous padding, no floor plane, no cast shadow outside the icon.
Subject: a compact quartermaster prep kit: brass-edged clipboard with blank parchment checklist, olive gear badge, small crate corner, wrench, armor plate, and coin token arranged as one clear emblem. Use a strong shield or tabbed-board silhouette so the icon reads as "prep" and "supply manager" at small size.
Style/medium: stylized 2D hand-painted game icon, warm parchment/brass war-room UI, thick dark outline, matte texture, kid-friendly and readable.
Composition/framing: square icon, centered emblem, three-quarter top view, no busy background, balanced negative space around the kit.
Lighting/mood: warm amber desk light, confident preparation mood, soft highlights on brass edges.
Color palette: parchment tan, olive green, muted brass, dark brown outline, steel gray tools, soft amber accents.
Materials/textures: parchment checklist, brushed brass clip, matte painted metal, subtle dust, simple rivets.
Text: no legible text; checklist lines only, no letters or numbers.
Constraints: no human face required, no real-world insignia, no flags, no political symbols, no weapon as the central object.
Avoid: generic shop cart, modern office clipboard, photorealism, neon glow, purple gradient, glossy 3D, clutter, tiny unreadable text, cropped tools.

## Acceptance Checks

- Reads as war preparation or quartermaster management before UI text is added.
- Distinct from supply crate, dossier, and codex silhouettes.
- Checklist area stays blank and clean.
- Strong enough silhouette for a 512x512 button background.

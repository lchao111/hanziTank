# Mechanical Wolf Pack Spritesheet Prompt

Runtime id: `wolfPack`
Current fallback: `assets/enemy-armor.svg`
Status: pending final bitmap spritesheet

Create a PNG spritesheet for a mechanical wolf pack elite in Hanzi Tank.
Style: kid-friendly toy-like mechanical animals, warm illustrated 2D, olive/rust/dark steel, amber highlights, thick readable outlines, matte texture, not frightening.

Subject: three small mechanical wolf-like units grouped as a pack, low armored bodies, tread-like paws, simple friendly angular silhouettes, visible rivets and brass joints. They face left.

Strict sheet standard: exactly 6 columns x 5 rows. Each cell is exactly 224 x 144 px. Total canvas size is exactly 1344 x 720 px. Use a solid pure green background, exactly `#00FF00`, across the entire sheet.

Do not include checkerboard, transparent preview background, grid lines, gutters, borders, labels, or frame numbers. Every wolf and effect must be fully inside its own 224 x 144 cell. Keep the pack within the central 70% safe area, preserve pack spacing across frames, use the same scale per row, and keep the same baseline/anchor within each row. No dust, blur, sparks, flames, explosion, or debris may cross cell boundaries. Keep each wolf readable but avoid scary teeth or realistic animal aggression.

Layout rows:
Row 1: movement/driving frames, pack idle prowl or tread-paw advance toward the player.
Row 2: normal firing/attack frames, staggered compact pounce/chomp attack with no wide FX.
Row 3: heavy firing/attack frames, flank dash or synchronized pounce with contained amber impact sparks.
Row 4: damaged frames, metal-spark hit reaction contained close to the pack.
Row 5: destroyed frames, pack break-apart destruction with small flames/explosion/debris contained fully inside each cell.

Negative prompt: horror wolf, sharp gore, blood, realistic predator, skulls, neon glow, photorealism, cropped pack members, inconsistent frame layout, transparent background, checkerboard background, grid lines, borders, effects crossing cell edges.

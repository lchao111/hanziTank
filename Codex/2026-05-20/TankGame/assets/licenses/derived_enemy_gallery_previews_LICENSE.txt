Derived Enemy Gallery preview assets for Hanzi Tank
Date: 2026-05-29

These PNG previews and interim spritesheets were deterministically derived from existing project-native bitmap spritesheets already stored in this repository:

- assets/sprites/enemies/enemy-tank-spritesheet.png
- assets/sprites/enemies/regular-enemy-tank-spritesheet.png
- assets/sprites/enemies/regular-infantry-spritesheet.png
- assets/sprites/enemies/grenadier-spritesheet.png
- assets/sprites/enemies/tank-dismantler-spritesheet.png

Process:

- Cropped transparent content from selected idle/attack frames.
- Refit the cropped bitmap frame into 256x192 transparent PNG gallery previews.
- Composed interim truck and elite gallery spritesheets from the same project-native bitmap frame sources.
- No SVG, CSS, canvas-rendered, or procedural placeholder art is used as final card art in the Enemy Gallery preview map.

Art status:

- Tank, Armored Tank, Regular Soldier, Grenadier, RPG Soldier, Heavy Soldier, and Tank Dismantler previews are derived from existing in-game bitmap spritesheets.
- Self-Destruct Truck, Spring Soldier, Drone Swarm, Mechanical Wolf Pack, and Mechanical Fleas previews are interim derived bitmap previews pending final generated/reviewed spritesheets from the prompt specs under assets/source/enemy-candidates/.

License/source note:

These derived files inherit the source status of the project-native spritesheets listed above. They are intended for this project and should be replaced for interim enemies once final reviewed spritesheets are generated and logged.
